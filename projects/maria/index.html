Maria listens — deeply and without judgement — then paints what cannot be said.

Maria creates soul portraits by spending time with people physically or virtually — allowing conversation, emotion, silence, and energy to guide the work.

These paintings are not literal representations. They are emotional and spiritual impressions often resonating deeply with others who encounter them.

Every piece holds space for connection. You may recognise yourself in a portrait not made for you — or feel drawn to one that speaks to something within.

This work emerged from quiet presence. Layers of colour hold emotion that was never spoken — shaped by listening rather than observation.
Created through intuitive response, this piece carries memory, spirit, and resonance. It invites recognition — not explanation.
Maria Grette creates spiritual and intuitive soul portraits, bridging art, presence, and human connection.
